# SQL_Exploratory_Data_Analysis
# 📊 Sales Data Analysis | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** using SQL to explore sales data, understand business performance, and uncover meaningful insights from customer, product, and sales information.

Through structured SQL queries, I explored the database, examined different dimensions, calculated key business metrics, analysed sales distribution, and identified top and bottom-performing products and customers.

The primary objective of this project is to demonstrate practical SQL skills, strengthen analytical thinking, and share knowledge of how SQL can be applied to business data analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore database structures, tables, and columns.
- Understand customer and product dimensions.
- Examine available sales date ranges.
- Calculate key business metrics and KPIs.
- Analyse sales performance across different categories and countries.
- Identify top and bottom-performing products.
- Evaluate customer revenue and order distribution.
- Apply SQL techniques to answer business-related questions.

## 🗂️ Database Structure

The analysis uses the `DataWarehouseAnalytics` database, which contains three tables under the `gold` schema.

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| `dim_customers` | Customer details, demographics, and geographical information |
| `dim_products` | Product information, categories, subcategories, and costs |
| `fact_sales` | Sales transactions, order dates, quantities, and sales amounts |

## 🔍 Analysis & Key Areas

### 1. Database Exploration
- Explore database objects using `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.TABLES`.
- Examine table columns using `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.COLUMNS`.
- Understand the overall database structure.

### 2. Dimensions Exploration
- Identify unique customer countries.
- Explore product categories and subcategories.
- Examine product names across different categories.

### 3. Date Exploration
- Identify the earliest and latest order dates.
- Calculate the available sales period in years and months.
- Identify the oldest and youngest customers based on birthdates.

### 4. Measures Exploration
Calculate important business metrics, including:

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Sales Amount
- Total Orders
- Total Products
- Total Customers
- Customers with Sales Transactions

Also, practise combining multiple business metrics into a single result using `UNION ALL`.

### 5. Magnitude Analysis
Analyse business performance across different dimensions:

- Customer distribution by country.
- Customer distribution by gender.
- Product distribution by category.
- Average product cost by category.
- Revenue generated by product category.
- Revenue generated by individual customers.
- Distribution of sold quantities across countries.

### 6. Ranking Analysis
Identify top and bottom performers through ranking techniques:

- Top 5 products by revenue.
- Top 5 subcategories by revenue.
- 5 lowest-revenue products.
- Top 10 customers by revenue.
- 3 customers with the fewest orders.
- Rank products based on revenue using `ROW_NUMBER()`.
